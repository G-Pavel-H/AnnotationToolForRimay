AnnotationToolForRimay

The human annotation app. Source of truth for all human data. Knows nothing about the LLM except one static snapshot.

Mongo Atlas→ CSV export→ RQ1ConvertToRimay (one-way — nothing flows back)
DATA where data lives CALC where numbers are computed UI what you click

Curation never overwrites. rimayText stays exactly as the annotator submitted it; your revision goes to refinedRimay with refinedBy/refinedAt. Both are in the export, so the experiment can be scored against either.
